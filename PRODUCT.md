# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Holidaymakers from Europe (German, Russian, French, British), usually staying one to two weeks, who want a car from the moment they land in Hurghada or delivered to their hotel or holiday apartment.
- Long-stay guests and expats who live in or winter in Hurghada and rent for weeks or months.

They browse on their phone, often before the trip or right after arrival, in their own language.

## Product Purpose

Hurghada Drive is a local car rental and private transfer business in Hurghada, Egypt. The website lets visitors see the real cars and their prices, work out a price for their dates, and send a ready-made request. Success is a WhatsApp request that the team can confirm.

## Positioning

A local company with its own cars, shown in real photographs, with fixed prices in Egyptian pounds and handover at the airport, the hotel or the holiday apartment. Long rentals get clearly cheaper weekly, two-week and monthly rates.

## Operating Context

- Primary action: request via WhatsApp (pre-filled message from the booking form). E-mail and phone are secondary.
- No online payment and no booking engine: a request is confirmed personally by the team.
- Handover points: Hurghada Airport (HRG), Hurghada city/hotel, El Gouna, Sahl Hasheesh, Makadi Bay, Soma Bay, Safaga. Delivery fee is charged once for drop-off and collection together.
- Use of the cars is only allowed inside the Red Sea Governorate; 150 free kilometres per day; minimum rental 3 days.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static website (HTML/CSS/JS), no server. Business data and prices live in `js/config.js`, translations in `js/i18n.js`.
- Five languages: German (default), English, Arabic (right-to-left), Russian, French.
- Prices in LE (Egyptian pounds), tiered: day (min. 3 days), 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month.
- Private transfers are part of the offer and must appear on the website. Transfer routes and prices are not yet supplied (open decision; never invent them).
- No insurance offer is advertised.
- GDPR-friendly: no cookies, no tracking, self-hosted fonts, map only after consent.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo (`img/logo.jpg`, wood background, green car, "Hurghada Drive – Car Rental & Private Transfer") is used unchanged, never cropped or redrawn.
- The owner asked for a dark, modern and professional look, with green (not yellow) as the accent, toned down rather than bright.
- Hero copy is the owner's own wording: "Dein Auto für Hurghada wartet bereits auf dich." and the intro sentence about handover at airport, hotel or holiday apartment.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photographs of two cars: Hyundai Avante and Kia Cerato, seven each (`img/cars/`).
- Real prices for both cars (`js/config.js`).
- Other fleet categories are placeholders until the owner sends the cars.
- No customer reviews, ratings, customer counts or press exist. Do not fabricate any.
- Contact data, address and legal pages still contain placeholders the owner must fill in.

## Product Principles

1. Real cars and real prices first: a visitor should see what they get and what it costs within seconds.
2. One clear action: WhatsApp request, available everywhere on the page.
3. Honest and plain: no invented claims, no hidden fees.
4. Works for a tourist on a phone in any of the five languages, including right-to-left Arabic.
